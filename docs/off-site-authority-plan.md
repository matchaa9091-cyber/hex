# Off-Site Authority Plan — Hex Escorts UG

Goal: close the authority gap with `ugandahotgirls.com` (registered 2019) and
`ugandaescorts.net` without paid links, spam, or adult-content policy violations.

Current baseline (17 Nov 2026):

- Domain registered May 2026; ~151 sitemap URLs.
- On-page work completed: top-10 city content, category hubs, FAQ/schema,
  breadcrumbs, profile schema, listing schema, sitemap alignment, fallback-safe
  noindex handling.
- Main gap is not crawlability. It is external mentions, brand searches, and
  consistent citations.

## Positioning

Be the **verified, direct-contact** Uganda directory. Every off-site mention
should repeat one of these proof points:

1. Photo-verified profiles.
2. Direct WhatsApp contact, no agency middlemen.
3. Coverage across Kampala, Entebbe, Jinja, Mbarara, Gulu, and regional towns.
4. Discreet, no client sign-up.

Use the same brand name, URL, and one-sentence description everywhere.

## 30 / 60 / 90 day plan

### Days 1–30

- Fix inconsistent brand descriptions across existing profiles.
- Create a reusable 50-word and 150-word boilerplate.
- Publish the four city guides and link them from the matching city pages.
- Claim 10 relevant directory listings.
- Set up a simple outreach tracker (site, contact, status, date, link).

### Days 31–60

- Pitch 20 travel/nightlife bloggers and 10 Uganda-focused podcasts or YouTube
  channels.
- Post 5 helpful forum answers.
- Add 5 complementary partner links.
- Refresh the city guides with new photos, FAQs, and internal links.
- Review Search Console for pages with impressions but low CTR; rewrite titles
  and descriptions before chasing more links.

### Days 61–90

- Double down on the 3 channels that produced real referrals, not just links.
- Publish one data-led piece: "Verified vs unverified escort listings in Uganda"
  or "Kampala outcall planning by neighbourhood".
- Request removal of broken or spammy inbound links only if they risk a manual
  action.
- Compare branded search volume and referring domains against the two
  competitors monthly.

## Link targets that make sense

Prioritise sites that already rank for Uganda travel, nightlife, expat, and
hospitality queries. Good fits:

- Uganda expat and relocation blogs.
- Kampala restaurant, hotel, and nightlife guides.
- Travel forums and city subreddits with promotion rules.
- Podcasts and YouTube channels covering Uganda travel.
- Complementary service directories (massage, wellness, nightlife).
- Local news or culture sites for comment/quotes, not paid advertorials.

Avoid:

- Bulk link exchanges and "SEO package" vendors.
- Adult link networks, PBNs, and auto-generated directories.
- Exact-match anchor spam.
- Links from unrelated casino, crypto, or pharmacy sites.

## Content that earns links

Each guide should have a clear search intent, original detail, and a practical
checklist. Suggested outlines:

1. **Kampala by neighbourhood** — Kololo, Nakasero, Ntinda, Bugolobi, Kisaasi,
   Muyenga, Munyonyo. Transport, hotel types, best times.
2. **Entebbe airport stopover** — arrival timing, nearby hotels, outcall
   planning, what to confirm before landing.
3. **Jinja weekend** — Nile lodges, transport from Kampala, evening planning.
4. **Safe booking checklist** — verify photos, confirm rates, agree incall vs
   outcall, protect privacy.

Each guide links to one city hub, one category hub, and two nearby locations.
That passes authority to the pages that need it most.

## Measurement

Track monthly in a simple sheet:

- Referring domains and new vs lost links.
- Branded impressions and clicks in Search Console.
- Organic sessions to city hubs and category hubs.
- Top landing pages by conversion to WhatsApp clicks.
- Competitor sitemap size and visible content depth.

Do not judge success by link count alone. The useful signals are branded
searches, direct visits, and WhatsApp clicks from city pages.

## Guardrails

- Keep all off-site copy non-explicit and platform-safe.
- Never promise verification or safety outcomes that the site cannot enforce.
- Do not buy links or use automated comment spam.
- Keep the public brand, URL, and contact details consistent.
- Re-check directory profiles quarterly for dead links and outdated copy.
